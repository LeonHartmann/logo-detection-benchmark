Reference images: dose and placement matter, not a free win. On the four brands with real sample sizes, attaching five per-brand reference collages to every call helped the small models (muse-glimmer +0.045, seed-2.0-mini +0.019 presence F1), did nothing for the frontier models (within noise), and hurt the VL specialist (qwen3-vl-plus -0.061, stripes recall 1.00 to 0.69). The one unambiguous effect: box quality halves for the box-strong models with refs in context (seed hit@0.3 0.485 to 0.257, qwen3-vl-plus 0.603 to 0.266, over all 242 truth boxes). Keep reference images away from whatever draws boxes. The apparent 11teamsports collapse under refs is not interpretable: that brand has exactly one truth frame, and the refs made models stop guessing (baseline precision was 0.17-0.25, mostly false positives). More 11teamsports truth frames are needed before any conclusion about the benchmark's weakest brand.

Zoom tool: modest, real, and rational. Letting models request up to three 2x crops of the same rung image lifted gemini-3.6-flash by +0.036 (into a tie for first), seed-2.0-mini by +0.029, and doubled opus's box hit rate (0.143 to 0.274, at 3x its cost). Models zoom more the lower the resolution (gpt-5.6-terra: 1.1 zooms per frame at 1080p, 2.2 at 144p); the specialist barely zooms at all. Zoom crops come from the same rung, upscaled, so these gains are attention gains, not smuggled pixels.

Per-brand calls: model-dependent, and the biggest surprise of the benchmark. Splitting each frame into five focused calls (one brand per call, each with only its own reference collage) wrecked four models: seed-2.0-mini fell 0.879 to 0.677, qwen3-vl-plus 0.944 to 0.773, gemini-3.6-flash 0.904 to 0.843, and claude-opus-5 and gpt-5.6-terra dropped to macro 0.68 and 0.67, all at 4-5x the cost. The failure mode is forced matching: asked about one brand in isolation, models declare something anyway, so dkh craters and delay precision falls; the other brands in a multi-brand prompt act as guard rails. But muse-glimmer-30b went the other way: 0.786 to 0.835 macro F1 (0.858 to 0.918 on the four reliable brands), making it the overall presence leader of the whole matrix. One small model genuinely benefits from focus while five others drown in it. Method effects are model-specific; test before you standardize.

Format compliance is part of capability. perceptron-mk1 (75 percent parse failure) and ui-tars-1.5-7b (own output dialect, F1 0.0) cannot be used through a JSON contract regardless of their vision quality. kimi-k3 and gpt-5.6-sol needed a 16k-token completion budget before their reasoning stopped truncating mid-answer; with it, both parse cleanly.

Caveats: OpenAI gpt-5.6 and Anthropic Claude 5 endpoints reject an explicit temperature, so those rows ran at API default while all others ran at temperature 0. 11teamsports has n=1 truth frames; treat its column as an anecdote. One of the 40 dataset frames has no completed truth label and scores as empty. Truth was pre-labeled by qwen3-vl-plus and human-reviewed box by box (provenance recorded per label file), which may slightly flatter that model's IoU scores.
